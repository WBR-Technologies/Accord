Meet Accord.
The chatbot where your key is the boss.

Accord is a chatbot & agent assistant — a cleaner alternative to Grok's bot — built on a BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) model. Plug in your own API key and you control the model, the cost, and the data. No subscriptions. No middleman. No lock-in.

Why Accord

Everything a hosted bot gives you — minus the hosted bot's grip on your wallet and data.

🔑

BYOK — your key stays yours

Paste your own API key and it lives in your browser only. It is never sent to our servers — because we don't have any. Your requests go straight from your browser to your provider.

🧠

Choice of models

Any model your provider offers, from fast-and-cheap to frontier. Swap models mid-conversation — Accord is a conduit, not a cage.

🔓

No lock-in

Static site, open code, OpenAI-compatible endpoints. Switch providers with a URL change. There is nothing to migrate away from.

🛡️

Privacy-first

No accounts, no analytics, no chat logs on our side. Your conversations are between you and the model you choose.

How it works

Three steps. About a minute.

  1. 1

    Paste your API key

    Grab a key from your provider (OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint). Paste it into the get-started section below. It's stored in your browser only — memory/localStorage — never sent to us.

  2. 2

    Pick a model

    Choose which model your key should use. Fast and frugal, or maximum capability — your key, your call.

  3. 3

    Start chatting

    That's it. Type a message and Accord sends it — with your key, from your browser, directly to your chosen endpoint.
